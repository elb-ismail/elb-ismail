A lamp that walks

The Moving Sanctuary

The world rearranges wherever no light falls. Walk your refuge across it, bring back whoever you can, and decide, every time, when to leave.

How it works
  • Unlit places change. A doorway can open or close only while no light touches it. Your map shows what you last saw, and it can be wrong. Beacons hold a place still.
  • Hollows move only in the dark. Lantern, beacon, flare and Sanctuary light freeze them. Focus the lantern to burn them. They come from where you are not looking.
  • Only what reaches the Sanctuary is safe. Gather ember and salvage, lead survivors home, then bank it all in the Sanctuary's light.
  • The longer you stay, the darker it gets. Each place thins out while the dark rises. Leave when the next place looks better than what is left here.
  • Some voices lie. Mimics pulse off the Sanctuary's heartbeat, drift when unwatched, repeat their words, and flinch from flares.
  • The world remembers. Beacons you leave keep burning, voices you abandon come back wrong, and your crew carries its scars. Nothing happens while you are away, and there are no timers, streaks or daily rewards.
